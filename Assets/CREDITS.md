## Voice

The voice clips in `Assets/voices/` were generated with
[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) text-to-speech, using the premade voice
**Jessica**, from the lines in `Sources/LiftoffCore/Resources/voices.json`.

They are sample clips for a non-commercial project. The lines mention the
author's own desk equipment; regenerate them with your own ElevenLabs account
(`liftoff-voices generate`) after editing `voices.json`, and review ElevenLabs'
terms for your plan before any other use.
